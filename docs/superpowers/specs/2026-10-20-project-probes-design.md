# Project probes: design

**Date:** 2026-10-20 · **Branch:** `feat/project-probes` · **PR:** #39 (next free number; changelog `pr: 39`)
**Status:** awaiting owner approval

## 1. Intent

The three projects (RuangRapi, This Flight-Sim CV, fuxx) currently live on the three planets: flying close locks you into orbit and opens the dossier. The owner thinks that is not a good home for them. The projects move onto **three drifting probes** that you **grab with the tractor beam**. Grabbing a probe opens its project popup, and after you close it the probe **fires its thrusters and moves somewhere remote**.

**Why (owner, confirmed in conversation):**
- Projects should feel like something you catch, not a sign stuck to a planet.
- It gives the universe more activity, and it starts the larger game vision (battles, achievements, divine coins; see memory `game-vision-combat-economy`).
- It frees the planets to be planets. The next stage after this one puts the real solar system in place, with real names and real distances.

**Owner-agreed points:**
- The probes look like old Voyager-style probes: photoreal and built in Blender, each with its own insignia, a blinking beacon light and a golden plate.
- Grabbing uses the existing tractor beam (F / pad LB / touch BEAM).
- After close, the probe moves somewhere remote but reachable. A radio line says it moved, and the radar finds it again.
- Keys 1–3, the waypoint and the radar follow the probes.
- The logbook keeps read projects re-openable.
- The first probe starts close to spawn.
- The planets lose docking and become scan-only.
- Plasma (left click) stays decorative in this stage. It becomes the gun in the later battle stage.
- Order of stages: **probes first, then the real solar system.**

## 2. Goals, non-goals, anti-goals

**Goals**
- G1. A recruiter reaches a project within seconds of arriving: one key press (or a short flight), then hold F.
- G2. Grabbing a probe is satisfying: the tether locks on, the probe is reeled in, the plate opens into the popup.
- G3. The relocation is visible and readable: a thruster burn, a radio line, and the radar reacquiring the probe.
- G4. No visitor loses progress. Dossiers already read stay read (`fitz-dossiers` keeps working).
- G5. The planets stay as beautiful as now, become solid scan-only bodies, and stop pretending to be projects.

**Non-goals (this PR)**
- The plasma weapon, combat, coins, achievements beyond the existing logbook.
- Real solar-system names, real distances, NASA maps, Venus. That is the next stage, with its own design.
- Making the planets chartable. `CHART_TOTAL` stays 5 on the wire, so no Worker deploy is needed and existing CARTOGRAPHER/AURUM holders are untouched.
- Any beacon-api change.

**Anti-goals (failure even if it "works")**
- A recruiter cannot find a project, or a probe parks somewhere unreachable or behind the edge haze (memory `no-unreachable-scenery`).
- A returning visitor's read dossiers reset.
- The beacon strobes. It must be a soft blink of ≤ 1.5 Hz with eased fades, not a hard on/off (the PR #28 gate review caught a 12 Hz strobe as photosensitive).
- Perf ceilings are raised silently. Any re-baseline must be deliberate and recorded in `docs/PERF-BUDGETS.md`.
- A probe that looks like a planet or a moon.

## 3. What the visitor experiences

1. **Spawn.** The spawn card reads roughly: "Three probes carry three projects. Grab one with the tractor beam (hold F) to open it." It also offers a fast path: "press 1, then hold F". The ship spawns facing probe 1, which floats a short flight ahead, with its beacon blinking in its project colour.
2. **Approach.** Inside the beam's range (30 u) and cone (20°), a HUD tip shows `PROBE IN RANGE // HOLD F TO GRAB`. The pad tip says LB, and the touch tip says BEAM.
3. **Grab.** The beam tethers the probe and reels it toward the ship. The probe is the lighter object, so it moves, as crystals do. When it reaches a few units off the nose, the scene freezes and the dossier popup opens, the same popup as today's.
4. **Close.** Esc, the X, pad B or a "RELEASE PROBE" button closes it. The probe fires a visible thruster burn, accelerates away for about 2–3 s, and then sits at its new remote spot. Radio: `PROBE RELOCATING // SIGNAL REACQUIRED AT <bearing/distance>` (wording is the builder's). The radar and the keys follow it.
5. **Logbook.** Each read dossier's essential becomes a button that re-opens the popup directly, with no probe moving. Unread ones keep a hint: `GRAB THE RUANGRAPI PROBE`.
6. **Planets.** Flying near one shows a scan card with real facts and a descriptive name. It has no lock, zone, dossier or gravity circle, and the ship cannot fly through it.

## 4. Design

### 4.1 The probe asset

- **One photoreal Blender model**, generated by script in `assets-src/` like the other assets, with provenance recorded. It has a high-gain dish, a foil-wrapped bus, an RTG boom, a magnetometer boom, a golden plate, and a small RCS nozzle cluster.
- **Telling the three apart:**
  - An insignia/trim zone is tinted per instance with the project colour (`projects.json` `color`).
  - An emissive beacon lamp is tinted per instance and blinked through instance colour. The glow comes from Bloom; there is no light.
  - My call: one shared mesh, not three models. Each probe's own insignia is a colour band plus the project's initial on the plate, if that fits the shared-material constraint. Otherwise colour only.
- **Rendering:**
  - One `InstancedMesh` of 3, one shared material, no `pointLight` (`LIGHT_BUDGET` 4).
  - Always mounted, so ShaderWarmup compiles it at load. The target is +1 draw call (+2 at most).
  - My call on what "no new programs" means:
    - The in-play programs tour must add 0, and every state must stay under its existing ceiling (79/84).
    - A probe material compiled during warm-up may add one or two programs to the load total. Record the new figure in `docs/PERF-BUDGETS.md`.
    - This allows a single material with a small `onBeforeCompile` patch for the zone tint and the emissive beacon. Splitting into two meshes is the fallback if the patch fights the sun-shadow patch.
  - The builder sets a triangle cap and pins it in `tests/assetContracts.test.ts`. It lands in the right asset pack.
- **It must not read as a planet or moon:** angular, small (about the ship's size to 2×), and metallic.

### 4.2 Where probes are and how they move

- **Home spots (unread).** Three fixed spots along the recruiter path from spawn, each reachable in a short flight.
  - Probe 1 sits a short flight ahead of the spawn heading, within easy beam reach after a few seconds of thrust.
  - Probes 2 and 3 are a little farther, spread out so the waypoint walks them.
- **Remote spots (after reading).** A fixed pool per probe (about 6–8), 150–420 u from spawn.
  - Every spot is inside `SYSTEM_RADIUS` (480) and out of the edge haze.
  - Every spot clears every planet and world reach, the career station, shipyard, gate and portal, the belts, and the crystal fields.
  - Each close moves the probe to the next spot in its pool (deterministic, cycling).
- **Persistence.**
  - New key `fitz-probes`: project id → spot index (0 = home). It is sanitised on load like the other keys: unknown ids and out-of-range indices fall back to home.
  - A read probe stays at its remote spot across reloads.
- **No orbit (my call).**
  - Probes hold position, joining UNIVERSE-RULES L16's list of station-keeping objects (stations, shipyard, shards…).
  - The honest nod is a faint periodic RCS puff. The builder adds probes to L16 in `docs/UNIVERSE-RULES.md`.
  - A slow tumble or idle spin is fine.
- **Clearance tests.** Every home and remote spot goes in `tests/planetClearance.test.ts` (or a sibling), and probes become crystal-field keep-out landmarks (`CRYSTAL_LANDMARKS`).
- **The relocation flight** is a visible animated burn (ease-in acceleration, plume puff) from the grab point to the new spot. It runs after the popup closes, once frames run again. The probe cannot be grabbed while relocating.
  - My call: the probe flies most of the way, then pops to the final spot outside view. It need not visibly cross 300 u.

### 4.3 Grab and popup state

- **Beam:**
  - `BeamKind` gains `"probe"`. TractorBeam considers probes within the shared `BEAM_MAX_TARGETS` budget.
  - The probe component consumes `"probe"` selections with a pull step, as FuelCrystals does.
  - The popup opens at an open distance the builder picks (outside the hull, a few units).
- **New open state:**
  - The popup gets its own store state, e.g. `openDossier: ProjectId | null`. It must not reuse `isOrbitLocked`/`activeZone`, because SpacePlanets rewrites those every frame.
  - Every consumer that today keys "dossier open" off `isOrbitLocked` must learn the new state. The code map lists about 12: `selectSceneFrozen`, Esc, pad B, the L/N/B key guards, the Logbook/Changelog panel auto-close, the letterbox, the TouchControls reset, the lock/unlock sounds, race abort, the beam off, the HUD ScanRing/BuoyCard hide and the waypoint hide.
- **Scene behaviour:** the scene freezes while the popup is open, as today. Held input is cleared on open, so F/W don't act under the modal.
- **Visit recording:**
  - Opening a dossier by any route records the visit through the same path as today.
  - `visitedDossiers` is filtered by **project ids**, not `PLANET_IDS`, so stored progress survives.
  - The "ALL 3 DOSSIERS READ // NEXT STOP…" line still fires after the last one closes.
- **Opening from the logbook** uses the same popup and state, does not move a probe, and needs no grab.
- **The popup component.** The inline dossier in `App.tsx` is extracted into a component (following `CareerDossier.tsx`) so the probe flow and the logbook share it. The copy changes from "ORBIT_LOCKED" / "THRUSTERS_BREAK_ORBIT" to probe wording.

### 4.4 Planets become scan-only

- **Internal ids stay** `ruangrapi` / `portfolio` / `fuxx`. Moons, textures, occluder slots, `PLANET_PROPS`/`PLANET_ORBITS`, displayAlbedo and the asset files all key on them, and renaming would churn every one. Only what the visitor sees changes.
- **Display names are descriptive, like the worlds' (P2):**
  - ruangrapi → `OCEAN WORLD` (earth type, 1 AU)
  - portfolio → `BANDED GIANT` (gas giant)
  - fuxx → `RUST WORLD` (mars type)

  These are my picks and are interim. The real-solar-system stage replaces them with real names.
- **Colours (my call).** `planets[].color` splits in two:
  - **Atmosphere colour:** keeps today's value exactly, including the banded giant's cyan atmosphere, so the planets look the same (G5).
  - **UI colour:** a new neutral colour drawn from each surface (builder picks). It drives the HUD, radar, scan card and the 3D orbit-path rings, so no visible ring is drawn in a project colour.
  - Project colours belong to the probes only.
- **What planets lose:** the zone, lock and dossier. They come out of `ZONE_PLANETS`, `WAYPOINT_PLANET_NAMES`, `JUMP_TARGETS` and `PAD_JUMPS`. The gravity-field circle meshes go, and so do the zone chatter lines keyed by project. Convoy chatter is fine.
- **Gravity stays:** wells, slingshot and `PLANETS_AND_WORLDS` are unchanged.
- **Solid.** Without the capture net, planets would become fly-through. World surface collision (`resolveWorldContact`) and camera keep-out (`keepCameraOutsideWorlds`) are generalised to the planets, moons included if they lack it.
- **Scan cards** keep `PLANET_REPORTS` facts, with the new names.
- **Not chartable**, not jumpable, still on the radar. This matches the five worlds.

### 4.5 Navigation

- **Keys 1–3 jump to probes 1–3** (wherever they are). Keys 4–6 stay career / shipyard / contact.
  - My call: a jump parks the ship facing the probe, inside beam range, and does **not** auto-open. The fast path is "1, then hold F". That keeps the grab as the moment.
- **Pad:** d-pad up/right/down go to probes 1–3, left goes to career. The `PAD_JUMPS` and `JUMP_TARGETS` tables re-point to probe targets.
- **Spawn and first press:** the spawn heading and `interceptAim` re-aim at probe 1 instead of `planets[0]`.
- **Waypoint:** the nearest unread probe, then career, then contact (the same `afterPlanets` order). It reads a live probe position, like `bodies`.
  - Builder's choice: a separate probe registry, or `bodies` with the pinned key test updated.
- **Radar:** probes are drawn as small project-coloured markers, with a visited ring when read. They are visible at all ranges, so a relocated probe is always findable.
- **HUD:**
  - The sector list becomes `SECTOR_PROBES — CLICK TO JUMP` with ✓ for read.
  - `DOSSIERS n/3` stays. The TARGET line names the probe.
  - Destination key numbering is unchanged (4–6).

- **Radio lines:** chatter lines that promise planet dossiers (`chatterLines.ts` 10, 13), plus new probe lines for in range, grabbed and relocating.
- **HUD strings.**
- **Logbook hints:** `logbook.ts` 27–29.

### 4.7 Live sky

There is no wire change and no Worker deploy. Ghost ships do not see probes. Probes are per visitor, so each pilot has their own.

## 5. Constraints

- **Perf (`docs/PERF-BUDGETS.md`):**
  - Deep-space calls have about 15 of headroom.
  - The beam-on and convoy checks are hard-capped at 110 calls.
  - There must be 0 new programs, and the programs tour must add none.
  - `LIGHT_BUDGET` is 4.
  - The e2e "modal open" state moves from a planet lock to an open probe dossier. Re-baseline it deliberately if its numbers shift.
- **Asset rules:** photoreal through Blender (memory `asset-quality-photoreal`). Originals go to `assets-src` and are pushed to its private remote.
- **Build order (my call):** the probe model comes first. Renders of it go to the owner for a look check before any gameplay is wired, because the asset is the costliest part to redo.
- **Tests to rewrite:** the planet-dossier coupling is pinned widely. The code map lists them:
  - **Unit:** projects, destinations, spaceStore, logbook, navigation, gamepad/padAction, orbitInvariant/orbitEscape/gravityWell/intercept, scannables, planetClearance, spaceWorlds.
  - **e2e:** transition, gameplay, mechanics, logbook, career, gamepad, programs, sky, touch, careerphone, beacons, fuel.

  Each rewritten assertion must keep its intent: for example, "a fast skim doesn't open a dossier" becomes "a fast skim doesn't stick to the planet, and the planet is solid".
- **New tests:**
  - probe spot clearance
  - relocation cycling and `fitz-probes` sanitising
  - the `"probe"` beam kind and open distance
  - logbook re-open
  - planet collision
  - progress kept for an old `fitz-dossiers` value
  - an e2e `probes.probe.mjs`: press 1 → hold F → popup → close → the probe relocates and the radar follows → reload keeps the spot
- **Verification bar:** unit tests, types, lint, the full e2e suite, and a real-Chrome look at the probe, grab and relocation. The owner sees screenshots before merge. The Cloudflare preview build must pass.
