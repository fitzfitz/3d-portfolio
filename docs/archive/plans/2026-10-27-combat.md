# The Nest (combat) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** One marked hostile zone, the Nest, below the orbital plane, where a visitor chooses to fight three waves of the PR #60 saucers and tardigrade with a plasma gun, can lose (towed out, nothing lost) or win (the Nest breaker badge and the CRIMSON plume).

**Architecture:**
- **Pure rules** (`src/utils/combat/*`, no React, no allocation per step): constants, hull, bolt pool and hit tests, aim, control mapping, the two monsters' AI, and one Nest state machine (`stepNest`) that owns waves, pools and the hull.
- **Per-frame state** lives in a mutable `combat` object (`src/store/combatState.ts`, next to `flight`); a small `combat` store slice changes only at events.
- **Scene:** a headless `NestDirector` steps the machine every frame and turns its events into store writes, sounds, radio and ship feedback. The always-mounted buoy marks the Nest; within 200 u a gate mounts the effects and, behind its own Suspense, the monsters (models loaded on demand, drawn through the monster lab's instancing API), which compile their programs before wave 1.
- **HUD:** the hull bar and wave readout in `HUDOverlay`, and a DOM `NestOverlay` (edge haze, hit flash, banners) costing no shader program.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, React 19, React Three Fiber 9, three 0.185, zustand, Vitest, the Puppeteer e2e harness (`tests/e2e/`).

**Spec:** `docs/archive/specs/2026-10-27-combat-design.md`

**Decomposition, adjusted from the brief:** the pure rules are three tasks (1 basics, 2 monster AI, 3 the Nest machine), since a reviewer can reject the AI while approving the hit maths; ship integration is split into the headless director with its ship hooks (Task 7) and the drawn scene (Task 8), so the state flow is reviewed before the rendering. The badge, plume and logbook counts join the store task (6) because `markNestCleared` records the badge.

## Global Constraints

- **Never an ambush.** Nothing hostile exists or acts outside the fighting area; a fight starts only on an inward crossing of the 70 u zone while the Nest is ready. Outside the Nest only the buoy, the radar marker, the edge haze (within 30 u of the zone edge) and the one rumour line appear.
- No gore, no explosions, no real death, no server changes, no coins, no new models, no new sound files. At most one new synthesized cue (`soundManager.plasmaShot()`); hull hits play the existing `impact()`.
- No new per-instance attribute on the saucer body (15 of 16 vertex slots used). Per-instance state goes through the existing `SaucerPose`/`TardigradePose` packing only.
- Reduced motion: `MONSTER_UNIFORMS.uMotion.value = 0`; state poses (aim, chase, fire, hit, defeat, charge, recoil, curl) still apply; idle blink 0; no camera shake; the hit flash is a static tint.
- No per-frame allocation in the combat loop: pools are fixed-length arrays of reused objects; vectors are module or component scratch.
- Draw calls: saucers 4 per LOD level in use, tardigrade 3 per LOD level in use; bolts (player, enemy and thrust glows) one instanced call; the plasma jet one call; smoke one call.
- `tests/storeShape.test.ts` changes only in Task 6, on purpose.
- Every PR has a changelog entry (`src/data/changelog.ts`, `pr: 67`).
- Before merging: `npm run build`, `npx vitest run`, `npm run test:e2e` in the main checkout (agent worktrees give false e2e failures).
- Subagents: never install packages; never kill a process by name; never run `wrangler deploy`.
- Copy, verbatim (`…` is U+2026):
  - Scan: `SCAN[THE_NEST] // HOSTILE LIFEFORMS · ENTER AT YOUR OWN RISK · F OR CLICK TO FIRE`
  - Rumour: `RADIO // SOMETHING IS NESTING BELOW THE PLANE. PILOTS REPORT SAUCERS`
  - Breach: `RADIO // HULL BREACH · TOWED TO THE BUOY · THE NEST IS STILL HUNGRY`
  - Cleared: `RADIO // NEST CLEARED · THE CRITTERS WILL BE BACK`
  - HUD: `ENTERING THE NEST`, `CONTACT IN 3…` / `2…` / `1…`, `CONTACT IN …` (waiting on warm-up), `NEST CLEARED`, `HULL BREACH`, `HULL: <n>%`, touch button `FIRE`.
  - Badge: id `nest`, title `Nest breaker`, hint and short hint `CLEAR THE NEST`, plume `CRIMSON` `#ff3b4a`.

## Rulings made while planning

- **The Nest's centre is `[-250, -390, 60]`** (494 u from spawn, 557 u from `DEEP_SPACE_POINT`). Found by a search against the real tests' helpers: 272 u from the nearest swept orbit (rule: `DEEP_SPACE_MIN` + the 110 u fighting area, stricter than the spec's zone radius, because monsters fly to 110 u), landmark margin 155 at radius 110, ≥ 222 u from every programs-tour stop (closest: a probe slot's remote spot), 484 u from the jellyfish path.
- **The buoy faces the Sun** (the inner system): `NEST_BUOY_POS = centre + 120 · unit(−centre)` ≈ `[-185.8, -289.8, 44.6]`. **The breach tow** lands 8 u past it (128 u from the centre), nose on the centre.
- **On-demand location:** `public/models-ondemand/` as loose files, outside `public/models/` (so no asset pack), fetched only by the Nest's load gate and cached by the service worker's same-origin rule. Not a fourth pack: the packs are startup downloads by design.
- **The game must not import `src/lab/`:** the builders move from `src/lab/labMonsters.ts` to `src/utils/monsters/buildMonsters.ts`; the lab imports them from there. A test pins it (FOLLOW-UPS' import-graph note).
- **"4 calls whatever the count"** means per LOD level in use (an InstancedMesh with count 0 draws nothing in three 0.185): saucers ≤ 7, tardigrade ≤ 6.
- **Click aim:** the click plane's hit point sits at the ship's depth, so a click aims at the point on the camera ray `|ship − camera| + 80` u from the camera. Clicks have no assist; held fire (F, LB, touch FIRE) does, and **the assist leads the locked monster** (`leadAim` at 140 u/s), so a strafing saucer is hittable.
- **Hit feedback** (sound, shake, flash, rumble) at most once per 0.25 s; damage is never throttled (the jet is continuous).
- **The jet hits** when the ship's centre is within 2.5 u of the jet's axis, 0–90 u from the mouth.
- **The rumour line** plays once per page visit, the first time the ship is within 400 u of the centre and outside the fighting area, not racing, radio free.
- **The fight pauses** (no movement, shots, damage or timers) in photo mode and while the logbook, patch notes or bottle panel is open. A dossier already freezes the frame loop.
- **Monsters spawn** 30 u from the centre on the side away from the ship (±10 u jitter); when a fight ends they **leave** by shrinking away over 2 s. Wave sizes are read from `isLowPerf` at each wave's start.
- **The jellyfish keep-out** is the fighting area (110 u + `JELLY_RADIUS`), not the 70 u zone, and the guide does not offer itself while `inNest`.
- **The edge haze and the hit flash are DOM layers** (like `EdgeHaze`), not a 3D shell: no shader program, and the haze is "drawn only within 30 u of the edge" by its opacity curve.
- **Thrust glows** under each live saucer are extra instances in the bolt mesh (still one call).
- **Sounds:** wave start `zap()`, Nest cleared `fanfare()`, firing the new `plasmaShot()`, hull hits `impact()`.
- **The new perf state is measured in the combat probe** (it already has the setup), not `transition.probe.mjs`, on SwiftShader with `lockFullFidelity`, in a debug formation so the frame is the same every run.

## Review Focus

1. **A crossing at warp or by a jump/teleport that lands inside** (39 u/s, or 300 u → inside in one frame): the fight starts exactly once and the HUD says `ENTERING THE NEST` once. Test in Task 3.
2. **Photo mode or the logbook opened mid-fight:** the fight pauses; nothing moves, fires or damages, and timers hold. Test in Task 3 (`paused`), wiring in Task 7.
3. **A tab hitch or a slow frame** (dt clamped to 0.05 s, a 7 u bolt step): bolts never tunnel through a saucer or the ship. Test in Task 1 (segment test) and Task 3.
4. **Low-perf flips mid-wave** (PerformanceMonitor declines during wave 3): the live wave keeps its monsters; the next wave uses the low-perf sizes. Test in Task 3.
5. **Several hits in one frame take the hull to 0** (two bolts, or bolts plus the jet): one breach, one tow, hull exactly 60, fight over. Test in Task 3.

Behaviour the tests pin (the implementer chooses the steering maths):
- Saucer: circles the ship at its `orbitR` (18–35) with its `orbitY` offset (−6..6), strafing round world Y, speed ≤ 14, facing slewed toward the ship at ≤ 3 rad/s; `alive` while idle/warn/burst.
- Attack (only while `shipIn`): `attackIn` (3–5, rolled at spawn and after each burst) counts down in idle → `warn` 1.0 s with `pose.chase` running 0→1 (wrapping), else −1 → `burst`: 3 enemy bolts 0.12 s apart from `sink.muzzle`, each `spreadDir(leadAim(muzzle, ship, shipVel, 48), 2°)`, `pose.fire` 1 → idle.
- `!shipIn`: no new warn, a running warn or burst stops; drift toward the centre until within 35 u.
- Every live or dying monster stays within `NEST_LEASH_R` of the centre (clamped after moving).
- `hitSaucer`: `hitT = 0.25` (`pose.hit = hitT / 0.25`); the 3rd hit → `defeated` (`pose.defeat` 1, drift away from the ship at 4 u/s) for 3 s, then shrink `scale` 1→0 over 2 s → `gone`. `leaveSaucer` → `leaving`, shrink over 2 s → `gone`.
- Tardigrade: swims toward the point 40 u from the ship on its side at ≤ 5 u/s (`pose.swim` = speed / 5), facing slewed toward the ship at ≤ 1.5 rad/s; attack every 6–8 s while `shipIn`: `charge` 1.6 s (`pose.charge` 0→1, still turning), then `jet` 1.2 s from `sink.mouth` along the facing at the charge's end, fixed (`pose.recoil` 1); `!shipIn` cancels a charge or jet back to `swim`. 18 hits → `defeated` (`pose.curl` 0→1 over 1 s, drift 4 u/s) for 4 s, shrink 2 s → `gone`.
